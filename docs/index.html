Get started in three steps

  1. 1

    Unzip the download

    Open your Downloads folder and double-click the ZIP file to reveal Clearhold.app.

  2. 2

    Move it to Applications

    Drag Clearhold.app into your Applications folder.

  3. 3

    Open Clearhold and sign in

    Open Clearhold from Applications. Sign in with your assigned username and password. No Tailscale setup is needed.

Need help signing in?

Recover access using the username and email associated with your account. Check your spam folder for the recovery email.
